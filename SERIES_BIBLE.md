# EduCrew Biography Series: Series Bible (locked from Episode 1)

Reuse these descriptions **word for word** in every prompt for every episode. Always attach
`characters_reference.png` as a reference image. Don't redesign anyone.

## Global rules
- 1990s India, middle-class Indian Muslim family, photorealistic live action, 35mm film look, warm Kodak colour, natural sunlight.
- **No faces are ever visible.** Use rear views, silhouettes, hands, over-the-shoulder shots and cropped framing.
- Vertical 9:16. No text, subtitles, graphics or music. Environmental sound only.
- EduCrew logo: original file, unchanged, lower-right corner, about 14% of the frame width, 4% margin, 90% opacity, visible for the whole episode.

## Characters
**LITTLE GIRL (the narrator as a child):** about 6 years old in Episode 1, about 115 cm tall, slim. Thick black hair in two shoulder-length plaits tied with red ribbons. Knee-length yellow cotton frock with tiny white flowers and puffed sleeves, black plastic sandals.
- She grows older across the series. Keep the same hair colour, the plaits and red ribbons (the ribbons are her signature) and the slim build.

**MOTHER:** early 30s, medium build. Long black hair in a low bun. Rust-maroon cotton salwar kameez with a mustard-yellow cotton dupatta over one shoulder. Green and gold glass bangles on both wrists.

**FATHER:** mid-30s, medium-tall, slightly broad shoulders, short neat black hair. Pale blue half-sleeve cotton shirt tucked into grey trousers. Steel-strap wristwatch on his left wrist.

**OLDER BROTHER:** about 10 years old, thin, short black hair. White cotton vest and khaki half-pants.

**GRANDMOTHER:** about 65, small and slightly stooped, silver-grey hair in a bun. Off-white cotton salwar kameez with a pale lavender dupatta loosely over her head.

## The home
- Whitewashed walls with a pale green lower half, red oxide floor.
- Ceiling fan, steel almirah, wooden windows with green iron grills.
- Inner courtyard with potted plants and a clothesline.
- Family meals on a floor cloth with steel plates and tumblers.

## Voices
- **Narrator (every episode):** adult female, Indian English, warm, reflective, understated. Use the same voice and settings every time.
- **Mother:** soft, warm, early-30s Indian woman.
- **Child:** a young girl's voice, playful.
